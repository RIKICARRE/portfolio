# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: the user left the choice to me. Astro (static output, built-in i18n routing for ES/EN, minimal client JS, deploys to any static host). Revisit if the user names a framework or deploy target.

## Users

Primary: recruiters and hiring managers evaluating the owner for a role. They skim, compare candidates quickly, and want to find credentials, real project work, and a way to make contact.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Ricardo (2026) to land a job. Current focus is cybersecurity: the owner is doing a master's degree in cybersecurity. Also open to software engineer and frontend roles, so the site must serve a security-first profile without hiding the engineering and frontend ability. Success: a recruiter leaves convinced of the owner's competence and contacts them or opens their CV.

## Positioning

A cybersecurity-focused candidate who also builds software and interfaces well; the portfolio itself is evidence of that frontend craft. (Confirm exact differentiator with the owner when real content arrives.)

## Operating Context

Bilingual site, Spanish and English, with a language toggle. Viewed mostly by recruiters on desktop and mobile, often briefly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual ES/EN.
- Education and certifications are presented together as a single unified section (one timeline or list), not two separate sections. New certifications are added over time, so the structure must be easy to extend.
- Sections known to be needed: unified education and certifications, project case studies, work experience / CV, contact.
- Undecided: domain, hosting, CV file format, contact channels, whether a blog or write-ups (CTFs, labs) are included.

## Brand Commitments

The owner has photo, logo, and brand assets on hand (not yet supplied to the repo). Binding: the structure of five TCP/IP layers (Aplicación, Transporte, Red, Enlace, Física) mapped to the page sections, and a retro VHS / green-phosphor CRT look (owner supplied a reference image).

## Evidence on Hand

The owner reports having: education history, certifications (growing), project case studies, work experience / CV, and photo/logo/brand assets. None are in the repository yet (project is empty). Future work must not invent employers, dates, certifications, project outcomes, or metrics; use clearly marked placeholders until real content is provided.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility over decoration: every claim traceable to real education, certification, or project evidence.
2. Legible in under a minute for a skimming recruiter; the path to contact and CV is always obvious.
3. Security profile leads, engineering and frontend ability is visible but secondary.
4. Education and certifications read as one continuous record of learning.
5. Both languages are first-class, not a translation afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement stated. Default to WCAG AA, and make the language toggle keyboard and screen-reader accessible.
