# Surface brief: home (redesign, replaces the key-counter world)

Mode: Experience. Single-page bilingual (ES/EN) portfolio home. Visitor: recruiter, skimming. Action: download CV. User pinned: modern, fluid scroll-linked motion where objects transform continuously and the visitor never feels page jumps (refs: rxresu.me, arko-sushi.midudev.workers.dev: smooth scroll, persistent pinned object, floating pill nav, dark ground).

## Direction contract

THESIS: One data packet persists on screen for the whole scroll and gets wrapped by a new layer per section (payload, transport, network, link); scroll is the cable. Refuses sectioned page jumps, hacker/matrix tropes, and a hero-with-photo template.
OWN-WORLD: Graphite ground (#0e1014). Layers are flat color-coded slabs in packet-capture colors: payload mint #9be3c4, transport periwinkle #8f9bff, network pink #ff9fb8, link amber #ffd36b; ember orange #ff6a2b reserved for what is live (en curso, CV, the sent packet). Bricolage Grotesque display, Geist body, Geist Mono for header-field data. Slab bands carry an RFC-style 32-bit ruler and field labels.
STORY: A recruiter sees a cybersecurity profile, watches the packet gain a layer per section (education/certs, projects, experience), and ends with the packet sent and the ember CV button.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left column: stamped name, role, intro, ember CV button. Right: the payload slab floating isometric in 3D with faint outer layers hinted. Floating pill nav on top with section links, language toggle, ember CV.
